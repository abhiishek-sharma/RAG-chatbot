The field of artificial intelligence was born in the 1950s. The term "artificial intelligence" was coined by John McCarthy in 1956 at a conference at Dartmouth College. Early AI research focused on problem-solving and symbolic methods. Researchers created programs that could play checkers, solve word problems in algebra, and prove logical theorems. In the 1960s and 1970s, AI research was funded by the Department of Defense, which was interested in developing intelligent systems for military applications. However, progress in AI slowed in the 1970s and 1980s, a period known as the "AI winter." This was due to a number of factors, including the limited computing power of the time and the difficulty of representing knowledge in a way that computers could understand. In the 1990s and 2000s, AI experienced a resurgence, thanks to the development of more powerful computers and new machine learning techniques. Today, AI is a thriving field with many applications in areas such as natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
